01 How it works

DPI looks for the site name. We cut it into pieces

Your ISP sees which site you're connecting to from the very first packet — the ClientHello. digital-bypass splits it so the filter can't put the name back together, while the site still can.

Without bypass

You DPI Site youtube.com RST · dropped

With digital-bypass

You DPI Site you tub e.com 200 · opened

  1. 1 Check the site

    In a few seconds the Check tab shows the cause: DNS spoofing, DPI on the site name, or an IP block.

  2. 2 Turn on what it suggests

    Under the verdict are buttons for the right modules: zapret2 or the proxy for DPI, Xray or Tor for IP blocks.

  3. 3 Browse

    Dots in the sidebar show what's running. Russian sites go direct in Xray and Tor.

02 Modules

Eight cases, one window

Each module handles its own kind of blocking. Not sure which one you need? Start with Check.

  • Case 01

    Check

    Compares DNS with DoH, tries TCP and TLS to every address and tells you exactly what's blocked — and what will help.

  • Case 02

    zapret2

    One-click DPI bypass via WinDivert, strategy tests for your sites and auto-tune. Works for every app, including games and Discord voice.

  • Case 03

    Bypass proxy

    Splits the ClientHello so DPI can't see the site name. No admin rights, no driver.

  • Case 04

    Xray

    Your own VLESS, VMess, Trojan and Shadowsocks servers and subscriptions. Russian sites go direct.

  • Case 05

    Tor

    For what's blocked by IP. Finds working bridges on its own and keeps Snowflake fresh.

  • Case 06

    Telegram

    A proxy for Telegram Desktop over WebSocket, with Cloudflare or your own Worker as a fallback.

  • Case 07

    DNS & DoH

    Switch DNS in two clicks, turn on DNS-over-HTTPS and check whether your ISP spoofs answers.

  • Case 08 new

    AI assistant

    An MCP server: Claude or Cursor checks the site and picks a strategy for you. If something breaks, it rolls back on its own.

03 For AI fans

Ask your assistant: “why won't Discord load?”

The built-in MCP server gives Claude, Cursor and other assistants real tools: diagnostics, strategy search, and control over the proxy, Tor and Xray. It's off by default and only listens on this computer.

  • AI can change settings only if you allow it.
  • Changes are on trial: without your confirmation everything reverts after 2 minutes.
  • Ready-made scenarios: “site won't open”, “Discord voice”, “my VPN server is blocked”.

06 FAQ

Interrogation record

Record · case 06sheet 1 of 1rec

00:00:14QuestionIs this a VPN?
Answer

Not quite. zapret2 and the bypass proxy don't route your traffic through someone else's server — they only keep DPI from learning which site you're visiting, so your speed doesn't drop. For IP blocks there's Xray with your own servers, and Tor.

00:01:02QuestionDoes it need admin rights?
Answer

For zapret2, DNS changes and hosts — yes: the WinDivert driver and system settings require them. The bypass proxy, Xray, Tor and the Telegram proxy work without them.

00:02:37QuestionWhy does my antivirus complain?
Answer

The app ships winws2.exe, the WinDivert driver, tor.exe and xray.exe — files like these often get flagged out of habit. Each one can be checked against its original source: links and checksums are in the README.

00:04:10QuestionWill it break banking sites?
Answer

It shouldn't. In Xray and Tor, Russian sites go direct by default, and zapret2 and the proxy only touch the sites on their lists. If a site breaks, add it to the exclusions.

00:05:48QuestionHow much does it cost?
Answer

Nothing. No ads, no sign-up. Xray servers are your own or from a subscription you pick yourself.
